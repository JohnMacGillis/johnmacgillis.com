The problem

Your growth isn’t limited by finding clients. It’s limited by the paperwork each one creates.

Every new client brings forms, letters, requests to third parties, follow-ups and reports. Most of it is done by hand, copied between a practice-management system, email, a fax machine and someone’s spreadsheet.

So you hire another assistant. Then another. Meanwhile requests slip, follow-ups never go out, and files sit waiting on something nobody chased.

I’ve seen it from the inside, and I’ve built the software that fixed it.

Featured work

An automated records desk for a large regional law firm

The problem
Staff at a firm operating in four provinces were preparing thousands of records requests to hospitals, clinics, insurers and government offices by hand. Whether a follow-up went out depended on someone remembering.
What we built
A system connected to the firm’s case-management software. It reads plain-language requests, matches them against a directory of 30,000 contacts, assembles the right forms with signatures, sends them, tracks replies, and follows up automatically with escalating letters.
The result
10,000+ requests sent in four months. Follow-ups send themselves, and the system alerts the team when something needs a person.

Built with a second developer as part of a 29-service platform designed to replace the firm’s case-management software.
