Featured work

An automated records desk for a large regional law firm

The problem
Staff at a firm operating in four provinces were preparing thousands of records requests to hospitals, clinics, insurers and government offices by hand. Whether a follow-up went out depended on someone remembering, and more clients meant hiring more assistants.
What we built
A system connected to the firm’s case-management software. It reads plain-language requests, matches them against a directory of 30,000 contacts, assembles the right forms with signatures, sends them, tracks replies, and follows up automatically with escalating letters.
The result
Follow-ups send themselves, and the system alerts the team when something needs a person.

Built with a second developer as part of a 29-service platform designed to replace the firm’s case-management software.
