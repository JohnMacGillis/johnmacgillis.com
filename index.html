About

Background

I trained as a journalist at Carleton University and have written for The Walrus and Financial Post. That's where I learned to write a script, frame a shot and get to the point.

I'm most useful to organizations spending real money on marketing without a clear line from spend to results. Professional services, regulated industries, and anyone trying to bring agency work in-house.

I'm based in Halifax and work across Atlantic Canada and remotely. I also build software on the side, including NS Legal Research and NS Hansard Search, so the data and automation side isn't a handoff.
